Want to Adopt?

Are you thinking about bringing home a forever friend? We are so happy you are here. At Furrever Pals, every puppy you meet in our classes is a rescue looking for their perfect match, and that match might just be you.

All of our adoptions are supported by our longtime partner, the International Dogs Rescue, an organisation dedicated to giving every dog a second chance at a loving home. When you adopt through Furrever Pals, you are not just gaining a companion. You are giving a rescue pup the fresh start they deserve!

Want to know who is available to go home? Check our puppy list!

Requirements

To make sure that all of our puppies are going to their forever happy home, our ideal candidates are expected to have plenty of patience and the ability to provide them attention and care when needed since all of our puppies are young and playful.

If you are a first time dog owner, you are absolutely welcome! We simply ask that you attend at least one of our puppy yoga classes beforehand so you can get a feel for caring for a young dog.

Adoption FAQ

All adopters must be 19 years of age or older. Those under 19 are welcome to adopt with a parent or legal guardian as the primary applicant.

Adopters must provide proof of a pet friendly living space, whether that is a owned home or a signed letter of permission from a landlord. All household members must be informed and in agreement with the adoption prior to submitting an application.

A brief home assessment may be required at the discretion of our team and our partners at the International Dogs Rescue to ensure the environment is safe and suitable for a puppy.

The length of the application process varies depending on each individual case. Larger households or more complex living situations may require additional time for review. Once your application has been assessed, we will reach out to the primary contact listed on the form with next steps!

Puppies are adopted regularly, which means our available list is constantly changing! If a puppy has left our list, it means they have found their forever home. We celebrate every adoption! Check back soon, as we are always welcoming new rescue puppies into our programme.

If you feel like you are the perfect match for the puppy of your choice, please fill out the application form below. We appreciate your interest and cannot wait to help you find your forever friend!
