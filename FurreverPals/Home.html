About Us

At Furrever Pals, we believe wellness is more than a workout. It's a feeling. Our puppy yoga classes are designed to nurture your body, ease your mind, and melt away the stress of everyday life, all while surrounded by the most joyful co-instructors you will ever meet.

Whether you are brand new to yoga or have been practicing for years, there is a place for you here. Furrever Pals is a space where community, movement, and pure puppy energy come together to create something a little unexpected and a lot good for you.

Come as you are. Leave feeling lighter.
